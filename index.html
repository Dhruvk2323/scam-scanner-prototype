💡 Golden Rule

When in doubt, don't click

Real companies and banks will never contact you out of the blue asking you to click an urgent link to verify a password or claim money.

3 Simple Rules to Remember

1
Ignore fake urgency

Scammers want you to panic and click before thinking.

2
Go directly to the website

Type the company's real address into your browser yourself.

3
Never share secret codes

No safe website will ask you to send your private login code via link.
